Inquiry privacy

Updated October 10, 2026.

Website inquiries

This static website does not submit your inquiry to a server or save form details. Selecting “Prepare review request” opens your email application with the details you entered. You choose whether to send that email.

Information you send

We use emailed names, business contact details, company information, and service requests to respond, assess fit, prepare quotes, and manage a requested engagement. Please do not send customer lists, payment card details, or sensitive personal information through the inquiry form.

Service providers

GitHub Pages hosts the website. Microsoft Outlook handles business email. The linked Square payment page is operated by Square. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy notices. This page uses no analytics script or tracking cookie set by Luminate.

Retention and requests

Our policy is to remove inactive inquiries from active business records after 12 months unless there is an ongoing relationship or a recordkeeping obligation. Provider backups and provider-controlled logs may follow different retention rules. To request access, correction, or deletion of inquiry information, contact luminatedigitaldesign@outlook.com.

Client customer data

Any access to a client's customer records, messaging tools, or scheduling systems requires a separate written engagement defining data access, responsibilities, permitted uses, and deletion or handover. This inquiry notice does not authorize customer messaging.
