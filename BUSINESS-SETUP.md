# Luminate Digital Design — Business Setup

## Offer
Luminate is a remote creative digital studio serving small businesses, creators and independent artists.

Core services:
- Web design
- Brand identity / logos
- Album & single artwork
- Artist branding
- Social graphics / posters / flyers

## Starting pricing
- Quick Design: $150+
- Signature: $500+
- Full Build: $1,000+

## Payment policy
Recommended starting structure:
- 50% deposit to schedule production
- 50% due before final delivery / launch
- Extra scope and revisions quoted separately

## Client pipeline
Lead → discovery → quote → agreement → deposit → production → review → final payment → delivery → testimonial → repeat/maintenance.

## Weekly sales target
Start with 10–20 qualified, personalized prospects per weekday. Prioritize businesses/artists where you can identify a specific visual or website improvement.
