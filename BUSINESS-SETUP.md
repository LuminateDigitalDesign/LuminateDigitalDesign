Updated October 10, 2026. Service brand under the existing Luminate Digital Design accounts; legal entity/DBA status is unverified. Launch with minimal costs alongside Nate's current job. First-year horizon: first 12 operating months after pivot, not the remainder of 2026.

## Business
Managed inquiry capture, estimate follow-up, team handoff, and reporting for independent home-service businesses. Start with one email workflow in the client's existing tools. Optional SMS/voice/AI additions require a separate technical assessment, scope, consent process, and client-funded tools. We have not deployed client integrations or proved outcomes yet.

Why this direction: Nate has home inspection and sales experience. The service addresses an identifiable operational problem and supports recurring contracts. Established vendors already provide these features; differentiation must be implementation, workflow maintenance, and measurable client-confirmed outcomes. No assumption that buyers will pay our proposed prices has been validated.

## Target customer
Independent HVAC, plumbing, roofing, and remodeling companies with sufficient existing inquiries, a responsible responder, spare service capacity, and job margins supporting the fee. Seek roughly 100+ inquiries/open estimates per month for the full offer; smaller volumes may justify only a pilot or a cheaper native-software solution. Expand worldwide where practical and permitted, using invited requests/referrals where unsolicited outreach is unsuitable. Exclude Nate's employer, its clients, confidential data, and competitors creating an employment conflict until Nate resolves that conflict.

## Sales and capacity assumptions
At an assumed 20% qualified-call close rate, 50 wins need 250 qualified attended calls, roughly five per week across a year. At an assumed 2% targeted-contact-to-attended-call rate, this needs 12,500 contacts, roughly 240 per week. These rates are unvalidated. A five-message hourly cap is not permission to bypass Outlook restrictions and is not a dependable sales engine. Use referrals, owner-led discovery, partnerships, and invited service requests. Track researched contacts, actual sends, delivered messages where verifiable, replies, attended calls, proposals, wins, collected fees, activation dates, and retention separately.

Nate must attend discovery calls, approve commercial commitments, and own delivery. Start with at most three concurrent pilots. Planning assumption: 15–25 implementation hours and 3–5 management hours/client/month; measure actuals before scaling. Fifty clients would require 150–250 monthly management hours plus 90–150 implementation hours for six new clients. This exceeds a solo part-time business. Hire client-funded delivery contractors from early collections, cap commitments to verified capacity, and add a delivery owner before scaling beyond ten clients. Do not promise staffing that is not contracted.

## Minimal-cost launch sequence
Days 1–7: interview ten qualified owners; confirm existing software and pricing pain; build a test-record demonstration; identify a delivery contractor and price a fixed pilot scope before collecting a deposit. Use free GitHub hosting and current Outlook/Square connections. No paid ads or subscriptions without Nate's authorization.

Days 8–30: sell and deliver up to three scoped pilots. Client supplies approved platform access, sample/test records, templates, recipient permissions, and scheduling rules. Baseline response time, open estimates, contactable records, booked estimates, and client-confirmed jobs. Launch only after acceptance and suppression/stop tests.

Days 31–60: review pilot economics and outcomes. Obtain permission for case studies. Convert only clients with adequate economic fit. At an illustrative 50% contribution margin and $3,000 management fee, six additional $1,000-contribution jobs cover the monthly fee twice; use each client's actual contribution margin, attribution, and incremental jobs instead of assumed sales revenue.

Days 61–90: expand only after repeatable delivery and retained clients. If ten qualified calls yield no paid pilot, revise the offer or price. If pilots produce no practical improvement or clients do not renew, stop scaling and investigate. Reforecast monthly using actual acquisition, activation, churn, collections, and delivery cost.
