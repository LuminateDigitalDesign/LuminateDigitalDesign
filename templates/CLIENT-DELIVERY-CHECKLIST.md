# Managed follow-up delivery checklist

- [ ] Written scope, contracting identity, payments and capacity agreed
- [ ] Deposit verified; client owns platform and approved access
- [ ] Baseline, volumes, record cap and contribution economics recorded
- [ ] Customer permission and suppression requirements established
- [ ] Test records only during configuration
- [ ] Templates approved; replies, declines and opt-outs stop automation
- [ ] Routing, duplicate prevention, record limits and escalation tested
- [ ] No unsupported promises, prices or job commitments in messages
- [ ] Client accepts configuration; setup balance reconciled
- [ ] Live activation approved; management period starts
- [ ] Weekly checks; incident log and client escalation
- [ ] Monthly report distinguishes messages, bookings, jobs and collected revenue
- [ ] Attribute outcomes conservatively; client confirms actual jobs
- [ ] Pilot review and separate continuation decision
- [ ] Handover, access removal and deletion follow agreed policy
