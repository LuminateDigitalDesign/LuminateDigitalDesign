# Luminate Outreach

Use only personalized, relevant outreach. Do not mass-spam or misrepresent the business.

## Structure
1. Identify a specific website, branding or visual opportunity.
2. Mention the specific observation.
3. Briefly introduce Luminate Digital Design.
4. Offer one useful next step.
5. Make it easy to decline.

## Example
Hi [Name] — I came across [business/artist] and noticed [specific observation]. I run Luminate Digital Design, where I build modern websites and visual branding for small businesses and artists. I had a couple ideas that could make [specific result] stronger. If you're open to it, I can send over a quick concept.
