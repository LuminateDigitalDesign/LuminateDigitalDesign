# New-offer outreach

Only qualified home-service companies with existing inquiries and potential follow-up needs. Verify the actual recipient and a current official source. Do not assert missed calls, lost revenue or broken follow-up without evidence. Prioritize invited requests, referrals and permitted communities worldwide. Respect country-specific requirements, opt-outs and provider restrictions; a public email address does not itself establish consent.

## Reply handling
For a positive reply: gather company, trade, tools, approximate volume, capacity and responsible responder; arrange owner-led discovery before quoting. Do not promise integrations already exist. For a legacy design reply: explain the new direction, honor any accepted earlier scope, and avoid automatically imposing the new price. Suppress opt-outs immediately. No uninvited follow-up merely because someone did not respond.

Log researched leads, sends accepted by provider, reconciled Sent Items, replies, attended calls, proposals, signed agreements and verified receipts separately. Messages in this file are drafts, not evidence of sends.
