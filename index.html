BEFORE WE WORK TOGETHER

Practical answers.

Is this a new CRM or an AI receptionist?

It is a managed implementation and follow-up service. We start with your existing software when practical. AI receptionist or SMS work is separately scoped after we verify the platform and requirements.

Will you generate new leads or guarantee sales?

The core offer focuses on inquiries and estimates you already have. Results depend on lead quality, your response capacity, pricing, and demand. No sales or revenue guarantee is included.

Can we start without expensive new software?

Often we can begin with an approved email workflow and a simple shared process. We verify this during the review. Any new subscriptions or usage charges require your approval and are separate.

What happens after the pilot?

We review the report with you. Continuing requires a new written scope and price agreement; the pilot does not automatically become a monthly subscription.
