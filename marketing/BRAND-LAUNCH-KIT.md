## Suggested launch sequence
1. Set business profile name, bio, logo and website.
2. Publish launch post with launch graphic.
3. Publish website concept with a specific design explanation.
4. Share local business post where group rules allow promotion.
5. Share album-art concept and artist post.
6. Show the inquiry-to-quote process.
7. Invite project inquiries; record responses and follow up individually.

## Outreach execution
Use the existing qualified prospect list. Verify each business and recipient before sending. Each message should mention one real, observed design opportunity. Track date, channel, recipient, response, next step and opt-out. Do not imply affiliation or invent results. Posts and messages in this kit are prepared, not published or sent.
